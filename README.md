# History Measure Power Lift Obstruction — Refinement Consistency Obstructs Nonlinear Power Lifts of Finite History Measures

This repository contains the source of the Cosmochrony working paper
*Refinement Consistency Obstructs Nonlinear Power Lifts of Finite History Measures*, a short
mathematical note succeeding
[Finite Criteria for Simplicial States and Local Tomography](https://cosmochrony.org/science/non-injective/states-effects-tomography/),
whose conclusion names an amplitude modulus as "the natural next step, outside this contract
entirely," and companion to the
[Projective Trajectory Branching](https://cosmochrony.org/science/cosmology/trajectory-branching/)
note, which supplies the automaton this paper specialises to.

## Central Question

A finite branching system induces, from an initial law, a canonical positive valuation on
admissible histories. A recurring move — reading such a valuation as an "amplitude" by taking
some power of it, canonically a square root — is rarely audited for its own sake. This note asks
exactly when a power lift of a branching valuation is compatible with the branching structure
that produced it, independently of what the lift is eventually used for, using the elementary,
theory-neutral compatibility condition of Kolmogorov consistency (marginal consistency of a
cylinder-measure family, as required by the Kolmogorov extension theorem).

## Core Results

- **Refinement-consistency theorem.** For any finite branching system, the power-lift family
  `mu^alpha` of its induced valuation is Kolmogorov-consistent, at a branching state reachable
  by a history of positive `mu`-valuation, if and only if `alpha=1`. The proof is an elementary
  comparison of `x^alpha` against `x` on `[0,1]`, strict wherever a state has at least two
  positive-probability successors — no phase, complex numbers, or Born rule involved.
- **Escort (row-renormalised) lifts, situated relative to the literature.** A row-renormalised
  power lift is trivially Kolmogorov-consistent for every `alpha`, but generally does not
  coincide with the pointwise lift once path-dependent normalisation factors accumulate. This is
  the same order relation underlying the classical escort-distribution/Rényi literature of
  nonextensive statistical mechanics (Abe 2000; Tsallis, Plastino & Alvarez-Estrada 2009), cited
  and situated here, not claimed as new in isolation.
- **Exact instantiation under H2.** Specialised, in exact `Q(sqrt(2))` arithmetic, to the
  non-backtracking Heisenberg $b$-shadow automaton of the trajectory-branching note, under an
  explicitly named, non-derived maximal-entropy/Parry postulate (H2). An explicit two-parameter
  counterfamily of primitive Markov kernels on the same support (recovering H2's own stationary
  law at one point) shows that support, primitivity, and the Pell recurrence alone do not select
  a unique kernel.
- **Endpoint/shadow quotient incomparability.** The endpoint quotient and the $b$-shadow quotient
  of the automaton are mutually incomparable, with explicit witnesses on words of length up to 4.
- **Exact relabelling automorphism and reality corollary.** Relabelling `X <-> X^{-1}` transports
  the Heisenberg endpoint by the exact group automorphism `(a,b,gamma) -> (-a,b,-gamma)`, from
  which it follows that every uniform-probe phase sum weighted by a real shadow-only valuation,
  over histories sharing a final `b_n`, is exactly real.
- **Bounded exact cancellation search.** Among all `13120` non-backtracking words of length up to
  8 (`q=17`), `64` classes share a final `b_n`, `62` carry genuine nontrivial relative phase, and
  none exhibits exact destructive cancellation — a calibrated negative result on this range, not
  a claim of non-existence at every length.

This paper establishes no Born rule, selects no complex carrier, and constructs no coherent sum:
Theorem 3.2's obstruction concerns classical cylinder-measure (Kolmogorov) consistency of an
*unnormalised* pointwise power lift, which an amplitude modulus is not in general required to
satisfy.

## Reproduction

All numerical and combinatorial claims are reproduced by one deterministic script, using exact
arithmetic throughout (Python's `fractions.Fraction` and a stdlib-only `Q(sqrt(2))` class) and no
third-party dependency:

```bash
python3 code/history_measure_enumeration.py
```

No floating-point computation enters any claim of the paper; no randomness is involved.
